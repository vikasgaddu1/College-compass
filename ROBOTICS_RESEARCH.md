# Robotics research review — October 14 and 16, 2026

Scope: the selected 15 universities, Fall 2027 entry, a North Carolina resident, and a balanced goal of hardware, controls, autonomy and supporting AI. Thirteen schools use the October 14 official-source review; Cornell and Johns Hopkins were restored to the list with that review. Rose-Hulman and Michigan-Dearborn were re-researched from current official sources on October 16, 2026. WVU is excluded.

## Recommendations

WPI Robotics Engineering is the strongest integrated project-based starting point for this goal; Michigan Robotics BSE is the broad public-university alternative; CMU Robotics BS is the research-intensive aspirational route through SCS. NC State Electrical Engineering, Controls and Robotics, is the strongest local-value route. These are qualitative curriculum/application recommendations, not personal admission or affordability predictions. RPI’s new primary ROBT degree is a credible robotics-first option with an explicitly required AI/ML choice, but established cohort outcomes and its accreditation status remain unverified.

## How to interpret the comparisons

Five selected schools have primary robotics degrees: CMU, Michigan Ann Arbor, Michigan-Dearborn, WPI and RPI. Purdue Polytechnic’s Robotics Engineering Technology BS is a separate applied technology option, not a sixth engineering robotics programme and not the recommended Computer Engineering degree. The explorer contains 16 credential entries for 15 schools.

The fit formula is 40% undergraduate robotics curriculum, 25% undergraduate robotics access, 15% hands-on projects, 10% foundations and 10% supporting AI. Missing dimensions are omitted and the remaining weights normalized; partial-evidence scores are less certain than fully documented ratings. Scores are analyst assessments on a 1–5 scale, not school-published rankings. A dedicated integrated robotics core scores 5; a structured ECE/CE route with robotics electives/credential and design work generally scores 4. Purdue CE has a lower evidence-based curriculum score because its current controls/robotics elective applicability could not be fully verified. ROET coursework and graduate robotics offerings are not borrowed to inflate it. A research institute’s breadth does not imply undergraduate course or lab access.

Admission tiers remain planning heuristics using the stated cohort and population. The 2015 CMU college comparison and explicitly inferred Georgia Tech computing rates are excluded. Unknown GPA weighting cannot shift a tier. Comparing early-round cohorts does not establish a causal benefit of ED or an easier major. Selected plans: CMU ED1; NC State, Northeastern, Purdue, Ohio State, UMD, RPI, Michigan and WPI EA (WPI EA1); Georgia Tech EA2; Cornell, Johns Hopkins and Duke RD. Rose-Hulman (EA Nov 1 or RD Feb 1) and Michigan-Dearborn (rolling; Dec 1 suggested, Apr 1 recommended) have verified dates but no round chosen yet.

### Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology

Recommend Computer Engineering (194 quarter credits, EAC-accredited) plus the Multidisciplinary Minor in Robotics, CPE track. Every minor student takes CSSE 120, ME/CSSE 435 and ECE 425; the CPE track adds CSSE 463 Image Recognition, ECE 320 Linear Control Systems (in place of ECE 380) and 8 credits of robotics electives such as CSSE 461, CSSE 415/416 or ME 445. CE majors keep the ECE 460/461/462 senior design; the three-term multidisciplinary robotics capstone is required only on the ENGD track, which corrects the earlier record. Admission is to the institute and students start their major in the first year; changing majors goes through the Registrar with no published GPA or capacity gate. There is no robotics BS and no named robotics research lab; undergraduate research runs through Rose Research Fellows and R-SURF, and robotics teams include Combat, MATE, Thornbots, Rover, VEX U and Rose Aerial Systems.

Fall 2027 dates: Early Action Nov 1, 2026 (non-binding, decision by Dec 15) and Regular Decision Feb 1, 2027 (decision Mar 15); $65 fee; GoRose or Common App; one recommendation; test-optional. Estimated 2026–27 cost about $86,344 on campus. The FIRST Robotics Scholarship is two $10,000-a-year awards, renewable to $40,000, applied for after admission by Feb 1. Still unconfirmed: elective rotations, whether ECE 425 and CSSE 463 can count toward both the CE degree and the minor, and whether the FIRST award stacks with merit aid.

### University of Michigan-Dearborn

Recommend the BSE Robotics Engineering (125 credits, EAC-accredited, launched Fall 2014). The College of Engineering and Computer Science admits first-years directly into their chosen major; students without Pre-Calculus credit start in Dearborn Engineering Success and must pass MATH 105 or 101 within three semesters with a 2.0 GPA. The required core covers embedded systems (ECE 3731), robotic manipulation (ECE 3641), mobile robots (ECE 4641), industrial robots (IMSE 381), controls (ECE 460 or ME 442) and the ECE 4987/4988 design capstone. Only about 7 elective credits remain for ML, AI or robot vision. The program is very small: 31 students enrolled in Fall 2025. An accelerated BSE/MSE in robotics is offered (3.2 GPA, up to 9 shared credits).

Fall 2027 admission is rolling with decisions from Oct 15, 2026; Apr 1, 2027 is the recommended deadline and Aug 15 the final one. The application is free (UM-Dearborn app or Common App) and test-optional. UM-Dearborn has no university-owned housing. The 2026–27 non-resident first-year budget is about $55,103 living off campus; the automatic Non-Resident Scholarship is $5,000–$12,500 a year by GPA. No Ann Arbor resources or outcomes are transplanted to Dearborn. Still unconfirmed: whether the 3.0 GPA admission guarantee covers out-of-state applicants, and how often upper-level robotics and AI electives run.

## Costs, aid and outcomes

Current verified COA figures mostly describe 2026–27, not the final Fall 2027 price. Record year, residency, programme, housing assumption, engineering differential, excluded insurance and transport separately. Georgia Tech has a published 2027–28 estimate. Michigan Engineering and Ohio State full COA remain unknown; billed Ohio expenses and LSA examples are context, not replacements. Scholarships have their own deadlines and eligibility, including separate applications and expired junior-year nominations.

IPEDS net prices are historical federal-aid-recipient averages, not a personalised NC robotics budget. Do not subtract them from current sticker prices to manufacture an aid award. First-destination numbers retain cohort, response/knowledge coverage, programme scope and mean/median distinctions. CS, Engineering or university-wide salaries/company bands are not robotics-major outcomes. Campus culture, Reddit sentiment, local travel context and old alumni bands were preserved with their original dates and scope labels rather than falsely reverified.
