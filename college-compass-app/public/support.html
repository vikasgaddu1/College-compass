Common questions

Why do I need a ChatGPT account?

The research and chat features run on ChatGPT. Signing in lets the app use your own plan directly from your phone, so there are no College Compass accounts or subscriptions. Usage counts toward your ChatGPT plan's limits.

Research stopped with a usage-limit message

Your ChatGPT plan has reached its limit for now. Wait for it to reset, or check Manage usage. Colleges already researched are kept; you can retry the rest later.

Are the odds accurate?

They are estimates, shown as a range, built from each college's published admit rate, test-score ranges and GPA data plus documented adjustments for your profile. They help you balance a list; they do not predict a decision. Tap any college and read "Why these odds" and the data notes.

A number looks wrong

Open the college, check the sources and data year, then tap "Refresh research". If it is still wrong, email us the college name and what you expected so we can improve the research instructions.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings → Export backup on the old phone, then Settings → Import backup on the new one. The file contains your answers, colleges, shortlist, notes and chat.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete everything, or simply remove the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
