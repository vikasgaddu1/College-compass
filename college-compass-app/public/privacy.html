Privacy policy

College Compass · Last updated October 16, 2026

College Compass helps high-school students build a balanced college list. It is designed so that we, the developer, never see your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter and everything the app produces is stored only on your phone (or in your browser, for the web version):

  • Your answers to the questions (home state, grades, test scores, interests, budget, income band, preferences)
  • Researched college data, your list, shortlist and notes
  • Your chat history with the Ask feature
  • Your ChatGPT sign-in tokens

We do not run a server that stores any of this. There are no College Compass accounts. You can export, import or delete all of it at any time from Settings.

What is sent to OpenAI

Research and chat are powered by ChatGPT. When you run research or ask a question, the app sends your profile answers, the names of colleges being researched and your question directly from your device to OpenAI's API, under your own ChatGPT account and subject to OpenAI's terms and privacy policy. Requests are made with the "do not store" option. The developer of College Compass does not receive, relay or see these requests.

Usage counts toward your ChatGPT plan. You can review it at chatgpt.com.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No third-party services other than OpenAI, which you connect yourself
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — we never have it

Children

The app is intended for high-school students, typically 14 and older, often together with a parent. Because nothing is collected by the developer, there is no account to delete; removing the app or choosing "Delete everything" in Settings erases all data.

Signing out

Signing out removes your ChatGPT tokens from the device. You can also revoke the app's access from your ChatGPT account settings.
